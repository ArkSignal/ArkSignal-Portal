The open channels in wide use on Meshtastic and MeshCore. Tick the ones to list, then choose where. A listed channel is public, shared on the default key, with positions no more exact than the public limit and no uplink or downlink; a community manager can change those afterward. Listing a channel also adds its name to the Public Channels setting, so the public rules cover it.

The FCC's 902 to 928 MHz rule asks for 500 kHz of bandwidth from digitally modulated systems. Meshtastic's 2.8.1 alpha already starts a new US node on LongTurbo from its own menu, while its apps still start on LongFast; MeshCore's US list is still 910.525 MHz at 62.5 kHz, with a 500 kHz proposal open. This list follows what the projects publish and can change; nodes only hear each other on the same settings.
