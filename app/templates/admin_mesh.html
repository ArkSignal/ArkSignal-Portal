Opening the Broker to Nodes (a step for the site's administrator)

Members make their own logins (My Mesh Nodes), and the mesh service puts each one in the broker: no commands. Two things are yours, once, because they are your certificate and your router:

  1. In Nginx Proxy Manager, make sure a certificate exists for the feed name ({{ conn.host }}; the one used by the proxy host for that name is fine). The broker finds it in the proxy's certificate store, copies it, opens its TLS listener and follows every renewal by itself: nothing to copy or restart. It shows TLS ready above when it has found it.
  2. On the router, forward TCP 8883 to the hub VM. The name is the feed name, which already points at your public address.

Nodes themselves need no open ports. The service can also read another broker instead (set in Settings).
