Run the ArkSignal Remote ID bridge next to your receiver and give it the upload address from the station's card. It passes the messages your receiver prints or sends over UDP on over https. The whole recipe is on How to Feed Remote ID.

cat /dev/ttyUSB0 | python3 rid_bridge.py UPLOAD_ADDRESS

Without the bridge, any program that can post text works: curl --data-binary @messages.txt UPLOAD_ADDRESS, one message per line, either the raw hex of a Remote ID message or a JSON object of what your receiver decoded.
