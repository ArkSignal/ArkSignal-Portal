Run the ArkSignal AIS bridge next to your receiver and give it the upload address from the station's card. It listens for the sentences your receiver sends over UDP and passes them on over https. The whole recipe, with AIS-catcher settings, is on How to Feed AIS.

python3 ais_bridge.py UPLOAD_ADDRESS

Without the bridge, any program that can post text works: curl --data-binary @sentences.txt UPLOAD_ADDRESS (one sentence per line, like !AIVDM,1,1,,A,...).
