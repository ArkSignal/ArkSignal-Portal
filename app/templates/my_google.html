My Google Sign-In

Link your Google account to sign in with one click, without typing your password. Your password still works.
{% call sec('link', 'Your Google Account', True) %} {% include "partials/_my_google.html" %} {% endcall %} {% call sec('how', 'How It Works', False) %}
  • Choosing Link My Google Account sends you to Google to pick the account. Google tells this site which account it is and that its email address is verified; this site never sees your Google password.
  • After that, Sign In with Google on the sign-in page signs you in as this account. If you use two-factor sign-in, the code step still follows.
  • The link is to the Google account itself, not to its email address. You can unlink at any time; an administrator can also remove the link if you lose access.
  • Only one Google account can be linked to a member, and a Google account can be linked to only one member.
