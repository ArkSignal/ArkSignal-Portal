Admin Google Sign-In

Let members sign in with a Google account they have linked. It stays off, and nothing shows on the sign-in page, until you have entered the client ID and secret from Google Cloud and turned it on.
{% include "partials/_admin_subnav.html" %} {% include "partials/_admin_google.html" %} {% call sec('setup', 'Setting It Up at Google (Once)', False) %}
  1. Open Google Cloud Console > APIs and Services > Credentials and pick or create a project. On the OAuth consent screen choose External, give the application a name and your support email, and publish it (sign-in with only the basic scopes needs no review).
  2. Choose Create Credentials > OAuth client ID, type Web application.
  3. Under Authorized redirect URIs add exactly: {{ redirect }} (it must be https on the real site).
  4. Copy the Client ID into Settings > Sign-In with Google > Google Client ID, and paste the Client secret into the box above.
  5. Set Sign In with Google to On in Settings. The button appears on the sign-in page and members link their account on My Account > Google Sign-In.
