Every flight with its times (each in its airport's own time), aircraft, notes and the people on board.

Download the CSV {% endcall %} {% call sec('import', 'Import a File', True) %}

The first line names the columns. Only from and to (airport codes such as LIT or KLIT) and a date or a departure time are needed; the rest is optional. A flight already in your diary (same airports and departure) is skipped. Up to {{ '{:,}'.format(max_rows) }} lines and 1 MB.

You can choose several files at once. A CSV, an AirTrail export, or tracks:

  • An AirTrail JSON export (in AirTrail: Settings, Export, JSON) brings everything: every time, the aircraft and airline, all the people with their seats, classes and reasons, and each flight's track. The CSV export leaves the people and the tracks out, but is understood too.
  • A GPX or KML track (a flight tracker's download, such as Flightradar24's KML) is attached to the flight in your diary it belongs to (same flight number, registration or airports, within a day), and becomes a new flight when none matches. A track can also be added on a flight's own page.

Columns the CSV file may have: {% for c in cols %}{{ c }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. Times are written 2024-08-15 12:15 in the local time of the airport they happened at. People are Name|Class|Reason|Seat|Where separated by semicolons.

Bring everything in straight from your own AirTrail: flights, people and tracks. In AirTrail make an API key (Settings, Security, API Keys) with the flights read scope, and the tracks read scope if you want the tracks. The key is used for this one import and is not kept.
