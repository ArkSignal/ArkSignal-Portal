Import a List From a Club or Council

For a repeater list a club, a repeater council or a coordinator has given you permission to use (RepeaterBook and RadioID lists cannot be loaded this way: their terms forbid it). A CSV file with one repeater per row; only freq is required, a city without a position is placed at the city, and repeaters already listed on the same frequency nearby are skipped. Download the template.

DMR Talkgroup and Contact Lists

The numbers behind the names: {{ dmr.talkgroups }} talkgroups and {{ dmr.contacts }} contacts are kept. They fill in talkgroup numbers when a code plug is imported. Bring a radio's exported Talkgroups and Digital Contacts CSV files, or a RadioID-style user list or BrandMeister-style talkgroup list; importing again updates what is there. To keep them updated automatically, put the list addresses under Settings > Repeaters and set the schedule on Data Updates (nothing is fetched from RadioID or BrandMeister until you have their permission: see DATA_SOURCES_TERMS.md).

Import a Radio Code Plug

A radio's channel list (the CSV a programming program exports, for example the DM32's) holds repeaters with their color codes, time slots, contacts (talkgroups) and tones. A channel on the frequency of a repeater already listed adds only what that repeater did not have (the DMR mode, the color code, the tone, the talkgroups); a repeater not listed is added waiting, without a position, for an editor to place. Simplex channels and channels not named after a repeater's call sign are skipped. The offset of a new repeater is the usual one for its band when the file does not give it.

The contact list gives the talkgroup names their numbers (columns such as Name and Call ID).
