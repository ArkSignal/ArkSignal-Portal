Admin APRS-IS Network

APRS-IS is the amateur radio network of servers that carries APRS reports; it belongs to the amateur radio community, not to this site. This page shows what this site reads from it (stations and weather around Arkansas) and what it sends to it (chat bridge messages and weather reports members chose to send). The settings are under Settings, APRS, Radio Bridges and Weather Stations. The read counts start again whenever the APRS service restarts.
