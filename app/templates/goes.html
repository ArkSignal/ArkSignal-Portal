GOES-19 (GOES-East) and GOES-18 (GOES-West) sit about 22,000 miles above the equator and photograph the Western Hemisphere every few minutes. Arkansas is seen by GOES-East. The pictures come from NOAA's public image server and are in the public domain; this site keeps the newest pictures and asks NOAA for new ones at most every {{ refresh }} minutes, only when somebody looks.

Want to catch the pictures with your own radio? Members with a weather satellite receiver can send their decoded pictures on My Account > My Satellite Receivers, shown on Satellite Images. More animations are on NOAA's GOES page. Source: NOAA/NESDIS STAR.
